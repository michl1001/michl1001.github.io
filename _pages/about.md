#### Hello! I'm Michael! A Roboticist and Embedded Systems Engineer.

I'm a Graduate in Robotics from the University of Pennsylvania (2025) and Computer Engineering from the University of California, San Diego (2023), currently working as a Real-Time Controls Software Engineer at OPEX Corp under their Warehouse Automation division.

My background is rooted in embedded systems, control systems, and backend development, complemented by experience in networking, system architecture, software engineering, physics mechanics.

My main interest is in resource-constrained robotics - to design, build, and control robots that don't have to rely exclusively on expensive, high-end hardware by exploiting the underlying controls and embedded systems.
